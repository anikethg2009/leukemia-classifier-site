Astraea Health

It puts microscope images of single blood cells in order, so the person checking them sees the most leukemia-like cells first.

Read on

Developed on 13,315 single-cell microscopy images from 73 people.

Runs entirely in your browser. Free, no upload, no account.

Presented internationally at a research collaboration in Austria

Disclaimer. This is a student research project and not a medical device. It has not been validated clinically, cleared by any regulator, or tested outside the single public dataset it was trained on. It does not detect or diagnose leukemia; it ranks pre-segmented single-cell images by visual similarity to leukemic lymphoblasts, for a qualified reviewer who examines every cell regardless. Nothing on this page or produced by the demo should be used to inform a clinical decision about any person.

Built while in high school. The dataset is C-NMC 2019, released for the ISBI 2019 challenge; we did not collect it. The model, the split, and the evaluation are ours. No tracking, no analytics, no cookies — the demo runs entirely in your browser.

Data: Mourya, S., Kant, S., Kumar, P., Gupta, A., & Gupta, R. (2019). ALL Challenge dataset of ISBI 2019 (C-NMC 2019) (Version 1) [Data set]. The Cancer Imaging Archive. doi.org/10.7937/tcia.2019.dc64i46r. Used under CC BY 3.0.
